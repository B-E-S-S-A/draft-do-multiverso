Multiverse Impostor

Everyone gets the same Marvel character except one player, who gets a similar one. That's the impostor.

Players
3

How to play
  1. Pick how many players (3 to 10). Each one holds their own card, peeks at their character without the others seeing, and lets go.
  2. On your turn, give one clue about your character without saying the name.
  3. The impostor doesn't know they're the impostor. They have to figure it out from the clues and blend in.
  4. After a few rounds of clues, everyone votes for the impostor and taps Reveal.
